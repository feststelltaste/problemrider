ProblemRider

A comprehensive catalog of common software development problems, their symptoms, and root causes — and the solutions that address them. Every problem links to the practices, patterns, and tactics you can apply when you are facing it. This resource helps developers, project managers, and teams identify issues, understand where they come from, and decide what to work on next.

Please note: this is an experimental project leveraging LLMs to generate interconnected problem networks. The app and content are provided “as-is,” without warranties of any kind. Use it at your own risk!
