## Completion Criteria

Check off a problem only after:

- every link in its **Symptoms** and **Causes** sections has been reviewed;
- direction, temporal order, mechanism, alternatives, and applicable scope have been assessed;
- applicable independent evidence has been recorded, with absence of evidence kept distinct from contradictory evidence;
- retained claims use qualified wording where the relationship is context-dependent;
- rejected, reversed, or corrected claims have been updated consistently at both endpoints; and
- `python scripts/check_links.py` and `python scripts/validate_causal_links.py --detail` pass for the edited batch.

A reciprocal link is structurally consistent, not causally confirmed. When an edge has already been reviewed from its other endpoint, reuse its evidence and decision but still verify the representation in this problem.
